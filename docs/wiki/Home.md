# Open Download Manager — User Guide

One queue for everything you download on Linux: files, torrents, videos, and full websites.

![Main window](images/main-window.png)

*The main window: status and categories on the left, downloads in the center, details at the bottom.*

## What you can do

- **Download files** with pause, resume, and automatic recovery after a restart.
- **Download torrents, magnets, and Metalinks** with file selection and tracker details.
- **Save videos and audio** from supported sites, pick quality, subtitles, and playlists.
- **Mirror websites** for offline reading with depth and filter controls.
- **Search torrents** without leaving the app and send results straight to the queue.
- **Stay private** with proxy, proxychains, and Tor routing.
- **Automate** with clipboard monitoring, folder watching, batch imports, schedules, and after-download actions.
